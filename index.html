I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Advanced Agent Lab at LG AI Research, working with Prof. Honglak Lee. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Andrew Perrault.

My research interests are in deep reinforcement learning with a focus on computer use agents.

Before my Ph.D., I earned my Master’s degree from the University of Southern California (2019) and my Bachelor’s degree from Inha University (2017) in South Korea, both in Computer Science.
